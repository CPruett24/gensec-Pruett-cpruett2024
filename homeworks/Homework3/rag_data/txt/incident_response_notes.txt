Incident Response Course Notes

The incident response process used in this example contains four phases:
1. Preparation
2. Detection and Analysis
3. Containment, Eradication, and Recovery
4. Post-Incident Activity

During containment, responders should preserve relevant evidence before making destructive changes to affected systems.

Logs should be collected from relevant systems when possible because they can help establish a timeline of events.

After recovery, the team should conduct a post-incident review and document lessons learned.
